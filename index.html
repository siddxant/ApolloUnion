Flagship Research Program

Detecting disease years before it appears

Standard risk models were built on Western populations. South Asians are systematically misclassified, and the consequences are clinical.

South Asians develop type 2 diabetes at lower body weight, cardiac disease nearly a decade earlier than European counterparts, and chronic kidney disease with almost no prior warning. The Framingham and QRISK models that most clinicians rely on were not calibrated for this population.

Apollo Union builds early-detection models using longitudinal laboratory data that captures physiological change over years, not a single cross-sectional reading. The result is a picture of where a patient is going, not only where they stand today.

17%
Estimated prevalence of chronic kidney disease among Indian adults. The majority remain undiagnosed until late stages, when intervention is far less effective.
  • Creatinine and eGFR measurements tracked over time reveal kidney function decline years before clinical symptoms appear
  • Models are trained and validated on Indian longitudinal data, not translated from Western cohorts
  • Results are publishable in high-impact journals and deployable across Apollo's hospital network from the first release
  • The same modelling framework extends directly to type 2 diabetes and non-alcoholic fatty liver disease

Research Programs

Six further lines of inquiry

Diagnostics

Indian Reference Intervals

Most laboratory normal ranges were derived from Western cohorts. A reference interval that misclassifies a healthy Indian as normal, or a sick Indian as borderline, undermines every diagnostic decision downstream. Deriving India-specific reference intervals from millions of laboratory results is a genuine scientific contribution that improves accuracy across every test type, carries low regulatory risk, and produces published results in months rather than years.

Near-term / Publishable

Pharmaceuticals

Real-World Evidence

Clinical trials establish whether a drug works in a controlled population. They rarely show how it performs in Indian patients, who may respond differently due to genetics, diet, and comorbidity patterns. Apollo Union generates real-world evidence on drug effectiveness and safety signals from prescription and outcomes data across a large, demographically diverse population, giving pharmaceutical companies the evidence their submissions and commercial decisions require.

Long-term / Core revenue

Global Health

Antimicrobial Resistance Mapping

India carries a disproportionate share of the global burden of drug-resistant infections. Combining culture test results, antibiotic prescription records, and pharmacy sales at scale reveals where resistance is emerging and which prescribing patterns are driving it. This is research fundable by global health bodies, publishable in the journals that shape international policy, and directly actionable by regulators and public health agencies.

High-profile / Fundable

Clinical Operations

Trial Recruitment

Finding eligible patients for a pharmaceutical trial typically takes months of chart review and site coordination. Structured clinical data makes it possible to identify eligible candidates in minutes. Pharmaceutical companies pay significantly for this capability, and the current state of manual recruitment is expensive enough that even a partial solution carries clear commercial value. This is a near-term product opportunity alongside the longer research programs.

Commercial / Near-term

Supply Chain

Pharmacy Demand Forecasting

Regional medicine supply is poorly matched to demand, producing stockouts in some areas and waste in others. Laboratory trends, prescription patterns, and seasonal signals make demand forecasting tractable at the district level. This is a practical product with clear commercial buyers in the pharmaceutical distribution and hospital procurement sectors, and it becomes significantly more accurate as the underlying data grows.

Operational / Commercial

Patient Safety

Prescribing Quality

Dangerous drug interactions, inappropriate antibiotic choices, and polypharmacy in elderly patients represent a substantial and largely invisible burden across India. Flagging these patterns at the point of prescription requires no new data collection and is clinically meaningful from day one. It addresses a problem that regulators, hospitals, and insurers all have strong incentive to solve, which makes it easier to deploy than most clinical AI applications.

Deployable / Clinical

The Data Foundation

Every product begins here

Each research program and commercial product rests on the same foundation: a structured, longitudinal view of Indian clinical data at a scale no other organisation has assembled.

The data already exists. It sits across thousands of diagnostic laboratories, pharmacies, and hospital systems, collected over years of routine clinical care. Apollo Union makes it legible, structured, and analytically useful for the first time at this scale.

What this enables is not generic machine learning applied to health data. It is population-specific medical knowledge, derived from the actual clinical experience of Indian patients.
